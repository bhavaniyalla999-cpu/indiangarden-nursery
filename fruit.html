Fruit Tree Growing Guide

Proven tips from Kadiyam horticulturists to maximize fruit yield

☀️

Full Sun (6-8 hrs)

Fruit plants require maximum direct sunlight for flowering and fruit development

💧

Deep Weekly Watering

Water deeply at base; reduce watering slightly during flowering to prevent flower drop

🪴

Organic Manure

Enrich soil with vermicompost and bone meal twice a year before flowering seasons

✂️

Canopy Pruning

Prune dead branches after harvest to let sunlight penetrate the inner canopy

🐝

Pollinator Friendly

Grow flowering plants nearby to invite bees and ensure high fruit setting

🛡️

Organic Pest Shield

Spray neem oil solution once a month to prevent leaf miners and aphids naturally

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about growing, fruiting cycles, and ordering fruit plants from Kadiyam.

When is the best time to plant grafted fruit saplings in India?

Monsoons (June to September) and early spring (February to March) are ideal planting seasons for fruit trees in India due to high soil warmth and natural moisture.

How soon do grafted fruit trees from Kadiyam start producing fruit?

Our grafted saplings (like Taiwan Guava, Kagzi Lemon, and Dwarf Mango) typically start flowering and yielding fruit within 12 to 24 months of planting.

Can fruit trees be grown in pots or terrace gardens?

Yes, dwarf varieties of Lemon, Star Fruit, Barbados Cherry, and Guava thrive in 18 to 24-inch containers with regular organic compost and full sunlight.

Does Our Kadiyam Nursery supply commercial orchard fruit saplings in bulk?

Yes, we supply wholesale orchard quantities with certified high-yield mother plant genetics and up to 40% bulk discount for commercial farm plantations.
