Indoor Plant Care Guide

Simple tips to keep your indoor plants thriving all year round

🌤️

Indirect Sunlight

Most indoor plants thrive in bright, indirect sunlight or filtered light near a window

💧

Mindful Watering

Check soil moisture before watering — allow top 1–2 inches to dry before watering again

🌱

Well-Draining Mix

Use quality potting mix with perlite and coco-peat for aeration and healthy roots

🍃

Foliage Care

Wipe leaves gently with a damp cloth monthly to clean dust and improve photosynthesis

🌿

Gentle Feeding

Apply dilute liquid houseplant fertiliser every 4–6 weeks during spring and summer

💨

Good Ventilation

Keep plants in airy rooms away from direct air conditioner vents and heat radiators

Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries about buying, watering, and maintaining indoor plants from Kadiyam.

Which indoor plants are easiest to care for in Indian homes?

The ZZ Plant and Snake Plant are the easiest indoor plants for Indian homes. Both tolerate low light, adapt to varying humidity, and need watering only once every 2-3 weeks.

Which indoor plants purify the air best?

Snake Plant, Peace Lily, Areca Palm, and Boston Fern are recognized by NASA clean air studies for effectively removing toxins like formaldehyde, benzene, and airborne impurities.

How often should I water my indoor plants?

Only water when the top 1-2 inches of soil feel dry. In general, water once a week during summer and once every 10-15 days during winter or humid weather.

Can indoor plants be shipped safely from Kadiyam across India?

Yes, our plants are safely packed in sturdy ventilated packaging with moisture-retaining coco-peat root balls to guarantee healthy arrival at your doorstep.
