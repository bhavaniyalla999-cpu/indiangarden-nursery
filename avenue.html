Avenue Plant Care Guide

Simple tips to keep your avenue trees thriving all year round

☀️

Full Sunlight

Most avenue trees need 6–8 hours of direct sun daily for healthy growth

💧

Deep Watering

Water deeply 2–3 times a week — avoid shallow daily watering to build strong roots

🌱

Rich Soil

Use well-draining soil enriched with organic compost for best establishment

✂️

Regular Pruning

Prune monthly to encourage bushy, healthy and disease-free new growth

🌿

Fertilise Often

Apply balanced NPK fertiliser every 45 days during the growing season

🪣

Mulch the Base

Mulch 2–3 inches around the base to retain moisture and suppress weeds

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about ordering avenue trees, spacing, and wholesale dispatch from Kadiyam.

What are the best avenue trees for roadside and highway plantation in India?

False Ashoka (Polyalthia longifolia), Mahogany (Swietenia mahagoni), Neem, Rain Tree, Royal Palm, and Gulmohar are top choices for Indian climate due to their deep roots, tall straight canopy, and wind resistance.

What is the ideal spacing when planting avenue trees?

For medium columnar trees like False Ashoka, 10–12 feet spacing is recommended. For large canopy shade trees like Mahogany and Rain Trees, 25–30 feet spacing allows full canopy spread without crowding.

Can Our Kadiyam Nursery supply thousands of avenue saplings for real estate or institutional projects?

Yes! We supply full truckloads of mature 6ft to 15ft avenue saplings and root-bagged trees directly from our 50-acre Kadiyam nursery farm with pan-India transport and wholesale discounts up to 40%.

How do you transport large avenue trees safely across states?

Trees are grown and prepared in heavy-duty polybags or root bags, watered thoroughly, and loaded using skilled farm loaders into ventilated trucks to prevent transit damage.
