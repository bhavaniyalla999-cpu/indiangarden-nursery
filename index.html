Fruit Plants Online — Delivered Pan-India from Kadiyam

Our nursery specialises in grafted fruit plants online including Mango, Guava, Lemon, Custard Apple, Star Fruit and more. All fruit plants are grafted varieties from certified mother plants, ensuring early fruiting and high yield. Ideal for home gardens, organic farms, and farmhouse bulk orders. We also serve government contract plantations and NGO green-cover drives across Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, and beyond.
