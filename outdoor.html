Outdoor Plant Care Guide

Simple tips to keep your outdoor plants thriving all year round

☀️

Full Sunlight

Most outdoor plants need 6–8 hours of direct sun daily for healthy growth

💧

Deep Watering

Water deeply 2–3 times a week — avoid shallow daily watering to build strong roots

🌱

Rich Soil

Use well-draining soil enriched with organic compost for best establishment

✂️

Regular Pruning

Prune monthly to encourage bushy, healthy and disease-free new growth

🌿

Fertilise Often

Apply balanced NPK fertiliser every 45 days during the growing season

🪣

Mulch the Base

Mulch 2–3 inches around the base to retain moisture and suppress weeds

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about ordering, caring for, and wholesale delivery of outdoor plants from Kadiyam.

What are the best outdoor plants for hot summers in South India?

Hardy varieties like Bougainvillea, Neem tree, Peepal tree, Queen's Crape Myrtle, Cycas Palm, and Areca Palms thrive in full sun and high temperatures once well-rooted.

How often should outdoor plants be watered?

Deep watering 2-3 times per week during early mornings is ideal. Shallow daily watering creates weak root systems; deep watering encourages roots to grow deep into the soil.

Does Our Kadiyam Nursery supply bulk outdoor plants for landscaping?

Yes, we specialize in bulk supply for landscapers, builders, villa communities, and resorts with direct farm loading from our 50-acre Kadiyam nursery and wholesale discounts of up to 40%.

How are outdoor plants packaged for interstate transport?

Plants are pruned, watered, secured in root bags with moist soil and compost, and stacked in ventilated crates or directly loaded into dedicated trucks to ensure fresh arrival.
