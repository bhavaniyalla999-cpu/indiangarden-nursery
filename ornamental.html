Outdoor Plant Care Guide

Simple tips to keep your ornamental plants thriving all year round

☀️

Full Sunlight

Most ornamental plants need 6–8 hours of direct sun daily for healthy growth

💧

Deep Watering

Water deeply 2–3 times a week — avoid shallow daily watering to build strong roots

🌱

Rich Soil

Use well-draining soil enriched with organic compost for best establishment

✂️

Regular Pruning

Prune monthly to encourage bushy, healthy and disease-free new growth

🌿

Fertilise Often

Apply balanced NPK fertiliser every 45 days during the growing season

🪣

Mulch the Base

Mulch 2–3 inches around the base to retain moisture and suppress weeds

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers about selecting, maintaining, and wholesale shipping of ornamental plants from Kadiyam.

What are ornamental plants and what are their best uses?

Ornamental plants are grown for aesthetic appeal, colorful foliage, architectural shapes, and fragrant flowers. They are ideal for borders, formal landscaping, lawns, patio containers, and topiaries.

Which ornamental plants are best suited for sunny tropical gardens?

Plumeria (Frangipani), Canna Lilies, Bougainvillea, Boxwood topiary, Crotons, and Cordyline (Red Ti) excel in direct sunlight and tropical heat.

Can I buy topiary and trained ornamental plants from Kadiyam?

Yes! We specialize in meticulously pruned cone topiaries, spiral topiaries, and boxwood balls, along with mature architectural palms ready for immediate installation.
